PRIVACY NOTE · 27 AUGUST 2026

Your session stays on your device.

Session Layout Capsule is a static, local-first tool. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or remote database.

What is stored

The layouts, links, MIDI cues, timers, and notes you enter are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. The service worker also caches app files so the tool can work offline. This data is controlled by your browser and is not sent to us.

What you share

When you export JSON, copy a handoff link, or print a QR code, the capsule data is placed in that file or link. Anyone you give it to can read and import it. Avoid putting secrets, license keys, or private credentials in a capsule.

Deletion and control

Delete an individual capsule from its card. To remove everything, clear site data for this domain in your browser settings. Export a JSON backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Network requests

The installed app only requests files from this site. Launch links are opened only when you choose them and are then governed by the destination’s privacy terms.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project repository linked from the product documentation.
